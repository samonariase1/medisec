Deploying SafeAccess Extension

Follow these 3 quick steps to load your Manifest V3 extension into Chrome:

1

Open a new tab and type chrome://extensions/ in your address bar.

2

Enable Developer Mode using the toggle switch in the top-right corner.

3

Click Load unpacked and select your project's extension/ folder.
